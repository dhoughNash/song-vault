Set up once

2 steps

1Open the menu

Tap the circle in the top right corner. You'll see Plans, Settings, Help and Sign Out.

2Fill in your info once

Tap Settings. Under My Default Info, type your name, your PRO, your publisher and your IPI numbers. Then tap Save my info. Every new song will start with these filled in.

Add a song

4 steps

1Start a new song

On the Song Vault, tap + New Song.

2Type the title and date

Type the song title and pick the date you wrote it.

3Add the writers and publishers

Type each writer's name, PRO and the % they own. Add each publisher the same way. Each list has to total 100%. Tap + Add Writer or + Add Publisher for more.

SESAC only: if a writer is with SESAC, the total line says "SESAC export: 50%". That's normal.

4Save it

Tap Save to Vault at the bottom.

Get your co-writers' info

4 steps

1Open the invite page

With your song saved, tap Invite Co-writers → at the bottom of the song.

2Send each co-writer their link

Under each name, pick Text, Email, Both or Link only. Then tap the send button. Your message is already written. Co-writers don't need an account.

3Tick off anyone you already have

If you typed in a co-writer's info yourself, tap ✓ No response needed on their card. The Vault stops waiting on them.

4Add their answers to your song

When a co-writer sends their info back, a box appears at the top of the Song Vault. Tap Merge into Vault to add it to the song.

Not right? Tap Dismiss instead.

File your songs

6 steps

1Open Export & Submit

On the Song Vault, tap Export & Submit.

2Download the file for your PRO

Find the box for your PRO and tap Export unsubmitted songs. A spreadsheet downloads. It only has songs you haven't filed yet.

Right now SESAC and The MLC are ready. BMI and ASCAP are coming soon.

3Upload it on their website

Tap Open next to the export (Open SESAC or Open MLC). Sign in there and upload the file you just downloaded.

SESAC: sesac.com → My Account → Song Registration → Bulk Upload. MLC: portal.themlc.com → Works → Bulk Upload.

4Mark the song filed

Back on the Song Vault, tap Mark and your PRO's name (and Mark MLC) on each song you uploaded. You won't file it twice.

5Send a song to your admin

Open Export & Submit, scroll to Email Admin, pick the song and tap Email Admin. Your email app opens with everything filled in. Tap Send there.

Your admin's name and email go in Settings under My Default Info.

6Mark it sent to your admin

Back on the Song Vault, tap Mark Admin on that song. It also marks your PRO and MLC as done.

Everyday things

7 steps

1Read a song at a glance

Blue means done. Red means still to do. The line under the title tells you the next step, like "Waiting on 1 co-writer" or "Needs MLC filing".

2Find a song

Type in the search box at the top of the Song Vault. It looks at titles, writers, publishers and more.

3Switch writers

Use the name box at the top to switch who you're working as. Tap + to add a writer. Tap Manage to turn one off.

4Copy a song

Tap Duplicate on a song, type a new title, and you have a copy with the same writers. Nothing marked as filed carries over.

5Not seeing something new?

Tap Reload at the top of the Song Vault.

6Back up your songs

Open Settings, scroll to Vault Backup and tap Export vault backup. A backup file saves to your Downloads.

7Free or Unlimited

Free holds 5 songs. Unlimited is $4.99 a month with no limit. Tap Plans in the menu to switch. You can cancel any time.
