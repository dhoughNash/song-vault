The SongSubHub

Your song filing cabinet. Save a song once, collect your co-writers' info, then send it where it needs to go.

1Add a song

On the Song Vault, tap + New Song. Type the title. Add each writer and the % they own. Add publishers too. Each list must add up to 100%. Then tap Save to Vault.

2Send co-writers a link

Open your song and tap Invite Co-writers. Pick text, email, or copy the link. Each co-writer gets a short page to fill out. They don't need an account.

3Merge their answers

When a co-writer hits Submit, their info shows up at the top of the Song Vault. Tap Merge into Vault to add it to the song. Tap Dismiss if it's not right. The gear shows a number when something is waiting.

4File it

Tap Export & Submit at the top of the Song Vault. Choose Export unsubmitted songs for SESAC or MLC. A spreadsheet downloads. Upload it on their site with the Open button. Back in the Vault, tap Mark SESAC or Mark MLC so you don't file it twice.

Good to know

  • The dropdown at the top switches which writer you're working as. + adds one. Manage turns one off.
  • Type a co-writer's name you've used before and their info fills in.
  • Duplicate copies a song so you don't retype.
  • Set your own name, IPI and publisher once in the gear under My Default Info. New songs start filled in.
  • Free accounts hold 5 songs. Subscribe in the gear for more.
  • Back up your vault in the gear: Export vault backup.
  • Not seeing something new? Tap Reload at the top of the Vault.
  • MusicMark exports (BMI, ASCAP) are switched off for now.
