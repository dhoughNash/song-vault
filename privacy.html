Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

The SongSubHub (the "Service") is a songwriter catalog management tool operated by Now You Know Nashville LLC ("we," "us," "our") in Nashville, Tennessee. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. Your email address and password when you sign up. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not stored in readable form.

Catalog and writer information you enter. Song titles, alternate titles, ISWC/ISRC/UPC codes, durations, genres, release dates, artist/album/label details, lyric excerpts, BPM, key, mood and instrumentation tags, and registration status (for example, whether a song was submitted to SESAC or The MLC).

Writer, co-writer, and publisher information. Names, IPI/CAE numbers, PRO affiliations, PRO account numbers, ownership shares, publisher names and IPI numbers, and, for co-writers you contact, email addresses and phone numbers. You are responsible for having the right to enter information about other people.

Settings. Your saved default info (name, IPI, PRO, publisher, label, distributor, and similar defaults) and, if you use the Email Admin feature, your admin's company name and email addresses, your CC address, and your email sign-off name. These are saved to your account so they sync across devices.

Co-writer submissions. You can share a link that lets a co-writer submit their own details for a song without creating an account. A co-writer who uses the link provides their name, IPI/CAE number, PRO, ownership share, publisher details, and optionally their email and phone number. That information is sent to the songwriter who invited them and held as a pending submission until the songwriter merges or dismisses it.

Subscription information. We store your subscription status (for example, active or inactive) and plan limits. Payments are processed by Stripe; we do not receive or store your full card number.

Technical data. Basic browser storage (local storage) is used to cache your catalog and settings on your device. Our hosting and database providers may log standard technical data such as IP address and request timestamps for security and reliability.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the Service: store your catalog, format and export registration files, and track co-writer outreach.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To sync data between your devices.
  • To respond to support requests and maintain and improve the Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your catalog data for advertising.

3. Service Providers and Third Parties

We rely on the following providers to operate the Service. Each processes data under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Supabase – database and authentication; stores your account and catalog data.
  • Netlify (or similar hosting provider) – serves the application.
  • Stripe – payment processing for subscriptions. Stripe receives the payment information you enter at checkout and an identifier linking the payment to your account.
  • Google – Google Fonts, and Gmail links that open in your own browser.
  • Content delivery networks (cdnjs, jsDelivr) – deliver code libraries used by the app.

When you export a file for SESAC or The MLC (or other organizations as support is added), the file is generated in your browser and downloaded to your device. We do not submit anything to those organizations on your behalf. What you upload to them is governed by their policies.

Text and email buttons, including Email Admin, open your own messaging or email app with a pre-filled message. We do not send messages for you.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger or sale of assets.

4. Data Separation

Data is associated with your account and protected by access controls in our database so that other users of the Service cannot view your catalog. Within your own account, you may maintain multiple writer profiles.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete individual songs in the app at any time. To delete your account and associated data, email us at the address below; we will process the request within a reasonable time, except where we must retain information for legal or security reasons. Backups may persist for a limited period afterward. Data cached in your browser can be cleared through your browser settings.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and managed authentication. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Use a strong, unique password.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You can access, edit, export (via the vault backup feature), and delete your catalog data within the app. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law, such as the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To make a request, contact us below.

8. Co-writers and Other Individuals

If you were invited to submit information through a co-writer link and want your details corrected or removed, contact the songwriter who invited you or email us and we will help route the request.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International Users

The Service is operated from the United States, and your information is processed and stored in the United States and other countries where our providers operate.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will change, and for material changes we will provide notice in the app or by email.
